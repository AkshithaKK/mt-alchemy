# MT Alchemy photo references

The website uses distinct editorial-style stock photographs for the Home, About,
Services, Upskilling, Store, Contact, course and product cards.

Images are loaded from their original Unsplash or Pexels CDN URLs in
`lib/widgets/ui.dart`; they are not bundled as local JPEG files. An internet
connection is therefore required for the photographs to appear.
